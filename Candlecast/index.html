Why no miracles

If someone promises you 99.9%, they're either lying or about to sell you something

Markets price in every piece of public information fast. If a predictable, exploitable pattern really existed, the largest quant funds in the world would have already found it — and by exploiting it, would have erased it.

Even the best quant funds operate with a razor-thin statistical edge — often 52-55% — turned into profit only through massive volume and risk management.

This project exists to learn: feature engineering, rigorous validation, honest backtesting. Real skills, regardless of whether the model "beats the market".

Can I use it for real trading?

It's built as an educational tool. Every decision is your own responsibility — check multiple sources and consider a licensed advisor.

Why isn't accuracy higher?

Because markets are efficient. Much higher accuracy on real data would almost certainly point to data leakage.

Does it work on any stock?

The code works on any Yahoo Finance ticker. Performance varies a lot by ticker and time period.

Is it free?

Yes, open source on GitHub. No hidden subscription.
