About

Candlecast is an independent, open-source project built to explore whether machine learning can say anything honest about short-term stock direction — and to be upfront about the answer either way.

It started as a learning project: feature engineering, walk-forward validation, backtesting with real trading costs. The 54.7% headline number isn't a sales pitch, it's the actual result of that process, folds and all.

There's no company, no fund and no paid product behind this — it's a solo, spare-time build shared publicly so other people can learn from it, poke holes in it, or build on top of it.

Privacy

This site is intentionally simple, and so is its approach to your data:

  • No accounts, no login, and no trading data or portfolio information is ever collected.
  • The newsletter form on the homepage is a front-end demo and does not currently send your email anywhere. If that changes, this page will say so before it happens.
  • If basic, privacy-respecting analytics are ever added to understand traffic, no personally identifying data will be sold or shared with third parties.

Cookies

Candlecast doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. The site is static, so there's nothing to log you into and nothing to remember about you between visits.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may set a technical cookie in your browser as part of delivering the font files — that's a function of the font provider, not something this site adds on top.
